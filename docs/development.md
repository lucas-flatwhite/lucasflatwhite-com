## Pages and Sections

- **Board (`/`, `#top`, `#work`)**: Paper page (`body.landing`). The name swaps one letter at a time for a cartoon picture of itself, every 1.4 seconds on every screen, and a pointer over a letter swaps it at once (`src/lib/name-swap.ts`). The board is one ordered list from `arrangeBoard` (`src/lib/projects.ts`): the book, then the featured projects with pictures, Daily Shot, the arcade box and the work badge, then numbered gaps. A project with `slot` in its frontmatter sits at that number and the rest flow around it; lucasflatwhite.com takes slot 07. Gap notes are tied to their numbers from slot 05 on, so a card taking a gap never shifts the notes after it. The book card (`src/components/BookStand.astro`, data in `src/data/book.ts`) is a CSS book on a pale blue ground: it sways and its cover peeks open now and then, and on hover it turns to the visitor and opens onto a first page with the Claude mark (`src/components/ClaudeMark.astro`). Its spine strip is cut from the cover spread. Pressing it opens the store list (`src/components/BookDialog.astro`, `src/lib/book.ts`): on a wide screen the closed cover lies in the middle and swings open, with the five stores on the right page; on a phone or a short screen the list rises as a sheet. Every store opens in a new tab. The address `/#book` opens it too, and the address and the book follow each other: going back from `/#book` closes the book, and closing it steps back over the `#book` entry it came in on (or only drops the hash after a shared link), so the homepage never sits twice in the history. Like the Work pictures, the book moves only while on screen and stands still for reduced motion. The two current pictures move: a crema ring spreads under glass panes, and a stack of glass windows turns from 2022 to 2026. They move on phones too, since neither redraws its glass: the windows are baked layers (`src/components/WorkYears.astro`) and the ring is a film (`src/components/WorkRipple.astro`) that loads after the page and only once its card is on screen, in AV1 where the device decodes it in hardware and H.264 elsewhere. They pause while off screen or in a hidden tab (`src/lib/work-motion.ts`) and stand still for visitors who ask for less motion, who never load the film. Every later picture stays still. After the projects come Daily Shot and the arcade box, then the work badge in slot 05, under the name on a four-column board. The work card (`src/components/WorkBadge.astro`, copy in `src/data/work.ts`) hangs my KakaoPay Insurance badge on its cord, on the company's yellow, with the company's own wordmark (`src/assets/brand/kakaopay-insurance.svg`, drawn in `currentColor` by `src/components/KakaoPayInsuranceMark.astro`). Its window answers like an AI (`src/lib/work-badge.ts`): each question is edited back to the part it shares with the last one and typed on, the answer streams in, and a 16 by 16 dot picture prints under it; a pointer asks how I see insurance instead. A pointer brushing the badge leans it away and a click presses it back, as damped swings added onto one layer. The words, dots and sway run only while the card is on screen (the same `data-motion` as the Work pictures) and not at all for reduced motion. Pressing it opens a short letter (`src/components/WorkLetter.astro`, `src/lib/work-letter.ts`) with the visitor's doubts, my answers, and a highlighted pledge. Questions type in, thinking dots pause, and answers follow in sequence inside a fixed, scrollable frame. Each active message reserves its final text dimensions and line breaks, so typing does not resize its bubble, move a word to the next line, or shift the window. Highlights wrap with the words around them. New messages scroll into view once, above the footer; manual scrolling stops that following until replay. The footer stays pinned to the bottom of the letter and links to `kakaopayinscorp.co.kr` in a new tab from the start of the conversation; Meet again appears beside it once the conversation ends and replays it. Screen readers receive the complete letter immediately. The browser's primary language selects Korean for `ko` and English otherwise. EN/KO controls stay at the top, and the selected language lasts until the page is reloaded. Reduced motion shows the complete letter immediately without typing or highlight animation. Daily Shot is a still thermal receipt (`src/components/DailyShotReceipt.astro`) for the AI notes I post every day; its DATE and TIME print as blank fields until the browser stamps the visitor's clock on load, then again each minute while it is on screen (`src/lib/daily-shot.ts`). The command menu leaves Daily Shot out, since Twitter there already opens the same profile. A click opens my X profile in a new tab, as the project cards do. The arcade box draws the lobby's paper handheld (`src/components/ArcadeHandheld.astro`) with a snake preview crawling on its screen while it is in view (`src/lib/snake-preview.ts`) and links to the lobby at `/arcade/`.
- **Arcade (`/arcade/`)**: Games live in [flatwhite-arcade](https://github.com/lucas-flatwhite/flatwhite-arcade), its own Vercel project. `vercel.json` rewrites `/arcade/*` to it, the way it serves `/ai-timeline-log/`. Snake is at `/arcade/snake/`. Beach Ball Merge is at `/arcade/suika/`, served by the independent [suika-balls](https://github.com/lucas-flatwhite/suika-balls) deployment at `suika-balls.vercel.app`. Its entry and asset rewrites precede the Arcade wildcard rules, and `/arcade/suika` redirects to `/arcade/suika/` so Godot resolves relative asset URLs correctly. Cache headers come from Suika, so successful release files keep their immutable cache while missing files can return no-store. The old `/play` address is gone.
- **Command menu (`Cmd/Ctrl + K`)**: Work, the book (`/#book`, so the arcade lobby opens it on the homepage too), featured projects, Arcade (the lobby), then the profile links (`src/components/CommandPalette.astro`). `src/lib/palette-commands.ts` builds the list once. `/commands.json` (`src/pages/commands.json.ts`) publishes the same list with absolute links and icons as path data, and the arcade lobby's menu reads it, so its links change with this site's deploy. `vercel.json` lets other origins read that one file, so the lobby also works on `flatwhite-arcade.vercel.app`. Changing the file's shape means raising `PUBLIC_COMMANDS_VERSION` in `src/lib/command-actions.ts` together with the arcade.

## Content and Code

- **Profile**: `src/data/site.ts` holds `siteProfile` and the homepage sections.
- **Links and icons**: `src/data/links.ts` defines external links. Each entry references a Phosphor icon from `src/lib/icons.ts` and a hover motion (`commit`, `cross`, `lift`). Links marked `featured: true` appear in the opening viewport; all links appear in the footer and command menu.
- **Photos**: Add one with `npm run photo -- <file> <slug> [--alt "..."] [--caption "..."] [--date YYYY-MM-DD]` on your own machine, then commit. `scripts/photo/` stands the photo upright, fits its long edge in 2000px, saves a JPEG of at most 600 kB, and strips all metadata, so no place or camera leaves with it. It writes the master to `src/assets/photos/` and an entry to `src/content/photos/`, both named after the month the photo was taken (`2026-10-podium`), and leaves the original where it is. Write the `alt` and `caption` in the entry if you did not pass them; the build stops while either is empty, and a caption is at most 40 characters, so it fits one line on a phone. `position` (`x% y%`) picks where the crop keeps its middle. The newest photo comes first. The card holds each photo for five seconds and fades to the next with its caption (`src/lib/photo-deck.ts`); the dots jump to a photo and a click on the card moves to the next. It pauses while a mouse or focus rests on it, off screen, and in a background tab, and stays still with reduced motion. The build cuts AVIF and WebP copies from each master, and the first photo loads at once because it is in the first screen. `tests/board-photos.test.ts` checks every master against the same rules, so run `npm test` before a pull request. Anything pushed stays in the public history, even after it is deleted.
- **Projects**: `src/content/projects/*.md`. `placeProjects` sorts `featured` entries with the highest `order` first. A featured entry with `art` gets a board box; `slot` pins it to a numbered board slot. A featured entry without `art` appears only in the command menu. `motion` is `ripple` or `years` for the two current pictures and is omitted for every later project.
- **Work pictures**: Run `npm run art` to regenerate `src/assets/work/*.svg` from the generators in `scripts/work-art/`. It drives the installed Google Chrome through `playwright-core` and embeds subset fonts in the years picture. The glass is drawn inside each SVG with filters (`scripts/work-art/glass.js`), not with `backdrop-filter`. The SVGs are the pictures' source; the page shows what `scripts/work-art/motion.ts` bakes from them into `src/assets/work/motion/`: the years picture as layers plus `years.css`, and the ripple as a still and a film per card size listed in `ripple.json`. Baking needs `ffmpeg` with `libsvtav1` and `libx264`; `npm run art:motion` runs it alone. The output is committed. A rerun with no changes writes the same SVGs, layers and CSS; the films differ by a few bytes.
- **Styles and design contract**: Design tokens live in `src/styles/global.css`, page styles in `src/styles/landing.css`. `tests/anti-slop.test.ts` enforces the typography, icon, and styling constraints across `src/`.

## Structure

```text
├── public/           # Static assets
├── scripts/          # The Work pictures (`work-art/`) and adding photos (`photo/`)
├── src/
│   ├── assets/photos/ # Photos beside the name
│   ├── assets/work/  # Generated Work pictures
│   ├── components/   # Astro UI components
│   ├── content/      # Project markdown and photo entries
│   ├── data/         # Site profile and links
│   ├── layouts/      # Shared page layout
│   ├── lib/          # Name swap, board and photo order, icons, and the snake preview
│   ├── pages/        # Route entry (/)
│   └── styles/       # Global tokens and landing styles
└── tests/            # Vitest source contracts and anti-slop checks
```
